Popular questions

🔥

How do streaks work?

Complete at least one lesson each day to keep your streak alive. Streaks reset at midnight local time.

Missed a day? Use a Streak Freeze from Rewards to save your flame.

⭐

What is XP and how do I earn it?

XP = Experience Points. You earn XP by finishing lessons, playing games, completing missions and keeping streaks.

XP levels you up and unlocks new content, badges, and avatar items.

🔒

Why is a lesson locked?

Lessons unlock one-by-one to ensure kids build skills in order. Finish the previous lesson to unlock the next.

Some bonus content unlocks when you reach an XP threshold.

🏅

How do I earn badges?

Badges are unlocked by real learning — completing sections, building streaks, finishing league weeks.

Each has a rarity: Common · Rare · Epic · Legend. See Rewards → Badges for the full list.

🏆

How do leagues work?

Leagues reset every Monday. Top 3 promote to the next tier, bottom 3 relegate down.

Tiers: Bronze → Silver → Gold → Emerald → Sapphire → Diamond.

⏰

Can I set screen time limits?

Yes! Go to Settings → Parent zone → Screen time limit. Kids get a gentle pause when they reach it.

Bedtime lock hides the app during sleep hours.

🔑

I forgot my password — what now?

Tap Forgot? on the Login screen. We'll send a reset code to the parent email on the account.

For kid safety, password resets always go to the verified parent email.

📡

Does Kidzio work offline?

Yes. Kidzio caches lessons locally so kids can keep learning on flights, road trips, or spotty Wi-Fi.

Progress syncs back as soon as you reconnect.

🗑️

How do I delete my account?

Go to Settings → Account and tap Delete account. All data is removed within 24 hours.

Or email us at privacy@kidzio.app.
